# Changelog

## v1.7
- Smooth bird flock: 16 frames at 16 fps in a seamless loop. Before, the flock jumped back about 6 px every second.
- Steadier crows: fixed shape and a gentler bank as they turn.
- Festival decorations removed.
- Tidied the Customize settings: heart rate, lake life and birds, drooping tree.

## v1.6
- **The tree** grows about 1.25x bigger as you near your goal, and **droops when you're behind on steps** (from 10 am).
- **Goal evening:** paper lanterns rise from the lake after 6 pm on days you reach your goal.
- **Nilgiri tahr** on the ridge some dawns.
- **Crows** some afternoons: they fly in from the left edge, wheel once round a full circle and fly away.
- **Edge bars:** two optional curved complication bars along the left and right edges, empty by default.
- **Customize switches** for heart rate, lake life and the drooping tree.
- The steps sign shows just the number, bigger.
- Fixes: tap areas no longer overlap, and 12-hour time shows "9:05" instead of "09:05".

## v1.5
- Bigger, glanceable layout: a large steps sign on the right edge (clear of the One UI 8 Now bar), a bigger battery plank, a 1.3x bigger boat.
- **Always-on:** the time grows and glides to the centre, then shrinks back into place on wake.

## v1.4
- Only the time, date, heart rate, battery, steps and bottles open apps; tapping elsewhere does nothing.

## v1.3
- **Always-on "still water":** the time with its live reflection on dark water, and smooth Watch Face Format 4 transitions.

## v1.1 – v1.2
- Nilgiri hills, illustrated clouds, Tamil Nadu seasons (konrai summer, monsoon clouds, lotus, cool-season storks and pelicans, morning mist).
- Unread notifications float in as message bottles.

## v1.0
- The living lake: real sun and moon (with phase), light on the water, dawn / day / golden hour / night, weather, the fisherman, fish, ducks and birds, the growing tree, the steps sign and the painted battery.
